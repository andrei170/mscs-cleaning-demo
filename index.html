What people ask before applying

Do I need a CV?

No. The six questions tell us what we need to know to decide whether to call you.

Why do you ask so many questions up front?

So we only call people the role genuinely suits, and nobody wastes an afternoon on an interview that was never going to work.

I have not used a water-fed pole before. Should I apply?

You can, but say so honestly. Experience with pure water systems is what we look for first.

Which areas will I work in?

London and Kent. Tell us which you can reach.

Will you contact my current employer?

No, not without your permission.

How quickly will I hear back?

If your answers match the role, we aim to call you soon after you apply.
